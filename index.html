FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about partnering with NetSpace Dev for your software engineering needs.

What types of software solutions does NetSpace Dev build?

We specialize in custom web applications, SaaS platforms, high-performance REST APIs, cross-platform mobile apps, and enterprise database systems. From initial system architecture and UI/UX design to full-cycle development and cloud deployment, we engineer software built for speed, security, and scalability.

What types of websites do you develop?

We develop high-performance corporate websites, SaaS landing platforms, client portals, interactive marketplaces, and custom CMS-driven web solutions. Using modern frameworks like Astro, React, Node.js, and robust PHP/MySQL backends, our websites achieve sub-second load times and peak search engine optimization (SEO).

What is your project timeline and delivery workflow?

We operate on transparent, weekly sprint cycles. A typical MVP or custom web application takes between 3 to 8 weeks depending on system complexity and third-party integrations. You receive weekly milestone demonstrations, private Git repository access, and live staging previews so you always know exactly how your product is progressing.

What engagement and pricing models do you offer?

We offer flexible models tailored to your requirements: fixed-scope milestone pricing for well-defined MVPs and project launches, or dedicated monthly sprint retainers for businesses needing an ongoing, high-velocity engineering team without the overhead of full-time hiring.

Do you provide post-launch maintenance and technical support?

Yes, every delivery includes dedicated post-launch warranty support to resolve any edge-case bugs and ensure smooth operations. We also provide long-term SLA maintenance contracts covering security patches, database backups, uptime monitoring, and ongoing feature updates.

Who owns the intellectual property (IP) and source code?

You own 100% of the code, intellectual property, design assets, and database architecture. Upon project handover or milestone completion, all source code and documentation are transferred directly to your organization's private repositories.
